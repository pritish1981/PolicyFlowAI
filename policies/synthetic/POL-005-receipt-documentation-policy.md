# POL-005: Receipt & Documentation Policy

## 1. Purpose

This synthetic policy defines minimum expense-documentation
requirements.

## 2. Mandatory Receipt Threshold

A receipt is **mandatory when an individual expense transaction is
greater than INR 500**.

Examples: - INR 450: receipt not mandatory under this threshold. - INR
500: receipt not mandatory because the rule is strictly greater than INR
500. - INR 501: receipt mandatory. - INR 7,000: receipt mandatory.

## 3. Receipt Content

A required receipt should reasonably identify merchant or provider,
transaction date, amount, currency, and goods or services purchased.

## 4. Missing Mandatory Receipt

An expense greater than INR 500 without its required receipt must not be
classified as fully compliant merely because it is below another
category limit.

## 5. Business Purpose

A receipt proves that a transaction occurred; it does not by itself
establish that the transaction has an eligible business purpose.

## 6. Electronic Documentation

A clear approved digital receipt is acceptable when it contains the
required transaction information.

## 7. Interaction With Other Policies

Receipt compliance is evaluated independently from amount-limit
compliance. For example, a domestic hotel expense of INR 6,500 is below
the POL-002 limit but still requires a receipt because it exceeds INR
500.
