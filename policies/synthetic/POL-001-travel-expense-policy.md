# POL-001: Travel & Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose

This synthetic policy defines general requirements for reasonable and
necessary business-travel expenses used by PolicyFlow AI.

## 2. Scope

It applies to approved business travel, including accommodation, meals,
and ground transportation. Category-specific policies take precedence.

## 3. General Requirements

Expenses must have a legitimate business purpose, be reasonable and
necessary, satisfy the applicable category limit, and include required
documentation.

## 4. Covered Categories

Hotel and accommodation rules are in POL-002. Meal rules are in POL-003.
Ground transportation rules are in POL-004.

## 5. Documentation

Receipt requirements are governed by POL-005.

## 6. Exceptions

Expenses above an applicable standard limit must not be autonomously
approved. Where an exception is permitted, a business justification and
human review under POL-006 are required.

## 7. Policy Precedence

An active category-specific policy controls when it provides a more
specific rule than this general policy.

## 8. Related Policies

POL-002, POL-003, POL-004, POL-005, POL-006.
