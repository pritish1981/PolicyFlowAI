# POL-002: Hotel & Accommodation Policy

## 1. Purpose

This synthetic policy defines standard hotel reimbursement limits for
approved business travel.

## 2. Domestic Hotel Limit

For domestic business travel within India, the standard reimbursable
hotel-room limit is **INR 7,000 per night**, excluding applicable taxes.

## 3. International Hotel Limit

For international business travel, the standard reimbursable hotel-room
limit is **INR 15,000 per night**, excluding applicable taxes.

## 4. Above-Limit Expense

A hotel expense above the applicable limit must not be automatically
approved. The employee must provide a business justification and the
case must follow POL-006.

Example: A domestic hotel amount of INR 9,500 is INR 2,500 above the INR
7,000 standard limit and therefore requires exception review.

## 5. Required Assessment Information

The assessment should include travel type, nightly amount, currency,
location, number of nights, business purpose, and receipt availability.
Missing information required to select the rule should trigger
clarification.

## 6. Documentation

Receipt requirements are governed by POL-005. Normal hotel expenses
above INR 500 require supporting documentation.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Charges

Clearly personal minibar, entertainment, or personal-upgrade charges are
not reimbursable unless another active policy explicitly permits them.

## 8. Related Policies

POL-001, POL-005, POL-006.
