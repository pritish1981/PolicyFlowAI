# POL-004: Ground Transportation Policy

## 1. Purpose

This synthetic policy defines standard reimbursement rules for ground
transportation used during approved business travel.

## 2. Airport Taxi Limit

For an eligible airport taxi trip within India, the standard
reimbursable limit is **INR 2,000 per one-way trip**.

## 3. Eligible Transportation

Eligible transportation may include taxi or app-based cab travel between
an airport and hotel or approved business location, plus reasonable
local transport required for business activity.

## 4. Above-Limit Expense

An eligible airport taxi expense above INR 2,000 per trip is above the
standard threshold and must follow POL-006 when an exception is
requested.

## 5. Required Assessment Information

The assessment should identify transportation type, amount, currency,
trip purpose, business purpose, and receipt availability.

## 6. Documentation

POL-005 applies. An airport taxi transaction greater than INR 500
requires a receipt.

## 7. Exclusions

Transportation primarily for sightseeing, personal errands, or other
non-business activity is not reimbursable.

## 8. Related Policies

POL-001, POL-005, POL-006.
