# POL-003: Meal & Daily Allowance Policy

## 1. Purpose

This synthetic policy defines the standard domestic meal and
daily-allowance rule.

## 2. Domestic Daily Limit

For approved domestic business travel within India, the standard meal
and daily-allowance limit is **INR 1,500 per employee per day**. This is
a daily aggregate limit, not a per-meal limit.

## 3. Eligible Expenses

Reasonable breakfast, lunch, dinner, and non-alcoholic beverages
incurred for approved business travel may be eligible.

## 4. Above-Limit Expense

If the eligible daily total exceeds INR 1,500, the expense is above the
standard threshold. Where an exception is permitted, the employee must
provide justification and the case must follow POL-006.

## 5. Documentation

Receipt requirements are governed by POL-005.

## 6. Exclusions

Personal entertainment, alcoholic beverages, and expenses unrelated to
approved business activity are outside this standard allowance.

## 7. Missing Information

If the date, daily total, travel type, or business purpose required for
assessment is missing, the workflow should request clarification or
return INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION.

## 8. Related Policies

POL-001, POL-005, POL-006.
